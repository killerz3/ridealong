# Ridealong brand

Name: **ridealong** (lowercase wordmark; "Ridealong" at sentence start). CLI/npm: `ridealong`.
Tagline: **Your agents ride along in your browser.**
One-liner: One logged-in Chrome for you and your AI agents. Self-hosted, per-agent tabs, workspaces that sleep when idle.

Logo: `brand/logo.svg` — a browser window; inside, a filled seat (you) and an outlined seat (your agent).

Palette (dark-first, matches the viewer UI)
- bg #0B0B0C · surface #141416 · border #26262A · text #EDEDEF · muted #8B8B93
- accent (amber) #FFB224 — the only loud colour; use sparingly
- agent colours (as in the viewer's avatars): teal #2DD4BF (hn-digest), green #4ADE80 (research-bot), violet #A78BFA (third agent)

Type: Geist (UI, headlines, tight tracking -0.03em on display sizes) + Geist Mono (code, URLs, CLI).
Fonts: `node_modules/@fontsource-variable/geist{,-mono}` in this repo — self-host, never load from Google.

Voice: plain, concrete, short sentences. No "revolutionary", no emoji walls.
Key beats: sign in once → agents work as you, each in its own tabs → you step in for captchas/2FA → workspaces sleep (0 RAM) and wake in ~2 s → works on your phone → self-host in one command.
